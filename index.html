This tool estimates a fair rent using a simplified version of a model trained on 973 rental listings across Aarhus. It looks at your apartment's size, room count, area and housing type to work out what a comparable listing typically costs.

The listings behind it are calibrated to match real Aarhus market statistics, average rent, price per square metre, and the gap between private and non-profit housing, rather than pulled from live ads. The result is an estimate, not a guarantee, so it's a starting point for judging a price, not the final word on it.

This estimate is indicative and should not be the sole basis for a decision to rent an apartment.
